Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Mailtrion ("we," "our," or "the app") is a service that helps you manage and interact with your email accounts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect an email account (such as Gmail or Outlook) to Mailtrion, we access:

  • Basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture) provided by the account provider during sign-in
  • Email data necessary to provide the features you use — such as reading, sending, or organizing messages, depending on the permissions you grant
  • Authentication tokens required to maintain your connected session

We do not collect more data than is required for the features you actively use, and we request the minimum set of permissions (scopes) necessary for those features.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core Mailtrion functionality (viewing, sending, and managing email on your behalf)
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To maintain and improve the reliability of the service

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your email content for advertising.

3. Google User Data

Mailtrion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage & Security

Authentication tokens and account data are stored securely and are only accessible to the systems required to operate Mailtrion. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only as long as your account remains connected to Mailtrion. You can revoke Mailtrion's access at any time through your Google Account permissions page (or your email provider's equivalent security settings), which will stop any further access to your data. You may also contact us to request deletion of any data we hold about you.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your personal data or email content with third parties, except where required to operate the service (e.g., hosting infrastructure) or where required by law.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
